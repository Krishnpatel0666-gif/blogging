BUYING GUIDE

How to choose the right laptop

5 min read • Laptop Basics

Choosing a laptop becomes easier when you first decide what you actually need it for. A student who mainly uses documents and browsers has different requirements from a gamer or video editor.

1. Start with the processor

The CPU handles general computing tasks. Modern Intel Core, AMD Ryzen and Apple Silicon processors can all provide strong performance. Look at the processor generation and class rather than only the brand name.

2. Check RAM and storage

RAM helps when many applications are open at the same time. For everyday student work, 8 GB can be workable, while 16 GB gives more comfortable multitasking. An SSD provides faster boot and application loading than a traditional hard drive.

3. Think about the display

Consider screen size, resolution, brightness and refresh rate. A creator may value colour accuracy, while a gamer may care more about a high refresh rate.

GAMING

What makes a gaming laptop powerful?

4 min read • Gaming

A gaming laptop combines a capable processor, dedicated graphics, fast memory and good cooling. The GPU is particularly important for demanding 3D games and graphics workloads.

GPU matters

A dedicated NVIDIA or AMD GPU can handle graphics workloads much more effectively than integrated graphics in many demanding applications.

Cooling matters too

High-performance components produce heat. Larger vents, multiple fans and good thermal design help a gaming laptop maintain performance during long sessions.

COMPARISON

MacBook or Windows laptop?

6 min read • Comparison

Both platforms support everyday productivity, programming, creative work and entertainment. The choice often depends on software requirements, ecosystem preferences, hardware options and budget.

macOS

MacBooks are closely integrated with Apple's ecosystem and use Apple-designed silicon in current models.

Windows

Windows laptops are available from many manufacturers, giving users a wide variety of designs, specifications and price points.

STUDENT

5 things students should check before buying

3 min read • Student Guide

Check portability, battery expectations, keyboard comfort, required software and warranty/service availability. A laptop that fits your actual daily routine is often more useful than one with the highest specification on paper.
